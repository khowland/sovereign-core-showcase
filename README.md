**Architect:** Kevin Howland  
**Domain:** AI Systems Architect & Security Consultant

## What it is
Sovereign-Core is a professional way to build and manage secure AI systems for critical missions. Think of this as a secure command center for high-speed information.

## How it's safe
It uses five layers of security and digital guardrails to move information safely across the country. We verify everything at every step to keep your systems secure.

## Why it matters
This shows how we use AI tools to move and manage data at light speed with complete control. We follow the strictest government security rules to protect your mission.

## The Five-Vector Schema
* **[St] Safe Vaults**: Permanent storage and clear logs that no one can change once saved.
* **[Lo] Safety Rules**: Strict rules to make sure data is always handled and moved safely.
* **[T] Analysis**: A high-speed engine that cleans and organizes data for smart tools.
* **[Ac] Action**: Fast tools that build and deploy systems automatically at light speed.
* **[η] Reliable Results**: Making sure technical data stays on track for important missions.
